V2 foundations + V3 customer intelligence

Marketing Intelligence Agent is a governed, proactive, role-based decision and orchestration system. Customer Journey Intelligence is a capability within the system, not its replacement. The boxes below represent architectural contracts, not live connected services.

Business-initiated

Objectives, questions, constraints, role-specific decisions; at most five clarifications, normally two or three.

AI-initiated

Proactive signal detection across business performance, customer needs, campaign systems and operational process gaps.

Architectural flow

01 · Intelligence & evidence contract

Translate the user or monitoring signal into a grounded business question, eligible audience, evidence, primary outcome and decision. Investigate across data sources; distinguish observed facts from interpretations.

02 · First-class Analytics-owned Semantic Layer

Canonical business KPI formulas and grains; customer × brand/category journey states; identities and approved joins; campaign/experiment mappings; semantic views and authorized deterministic query capabilities; provenance, quality, privacy, freshness and access policies. No unrestricted SQL for business users. Analytics approves definitions and changes.

03 · Hypothesis Intelligence

Generate competing explanations, cross-check supporting and contradicting evidence, then prioritize two to three hypotheses by confidence, customer impact, actionability, risk and testability. An anomaly is not proof of cause.

04 · Small-List Testing Engine

Offer one to three smallest useful validation steps or intervention experiments with eligibility, comparison/holdout, primary and leading KPIs, guardrails and decisions. Hypothesis validation and causal intervention testing are separate.

05 · Analytics validation and Business/CX judgment

Analytics approves evidence and methodology; business owners choose priorities and authorize material actions. AI narrows the decision space; humans remain accountable.

06 · Bidirectional Orchestration & Health

Outbound: scoped signals, recommendations, drafts and human-approved actions through a separate connector/API/workflow boundary to existing systems. Inbound: acknowledgement IDs, configuration, delivery status, audience overlap, suppression/consent, tracking/experiment health, customer response and downstream outcomes. Detect unexecuted recommendations and missing measurement; escalate failures.

07 · Existing systems execute

Warehouse, web/app, CRM/CDP, email/MAP, paid media, commerce, experimentation and support/VOC remain systems of record. Permission ladder: Read → Recommend → Draft → Human-approved Execute.

08 · Golden Insights & Continuous Learning

Independent, cross-cutting evaluation of meaningful insight detection, evidence use, competing hypotheses, small-test quality, tool/permission integrity, operational health and supported outcome claims. Golden questions, regression checks and autonomy gates operate across all stages, not merely at the end.

Business / Customer Outcome Loop

Signal → test/action → execution telemetry → customer outcome and guardrails → validated impact → next decision.

Agent Intelligence Quality Loop

Evidence/hypothesis/test quality → Golden Insights evaluation → error analysis → governed improvements to reasoning, capabilities and tests.

Separate knowledge from semantics

Semantic Layer: defines trusted business facts, joins and measurements. Knowledge / Experiment & Decision Memory: preserves conditional learning, evidence, approved actions, external execution IDs, results, gaps and revalidation needs. Neither may be silently rewritten by the reasoning model.
