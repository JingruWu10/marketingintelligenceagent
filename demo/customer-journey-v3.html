Original 12-step closed-loop architecture

01–03 · Context → Evidence

1 Business question & context → 2 AI evidence contract → 3 governed sources and deterministic analytics.

04–07 · Intelligence

4 Continuous monitoring → 5 cross-source diagnosis → 6 evidence-ranked hypotheses → 7 smallest useful proposed test or action.

08–10 · Decide & Orchestrate

8 Analytics validates and Business/CX decides → 9 separate read/recommend/draft/approved-write orchestration layer → 10 existing CRM, CDP, campaign, web and experimentation systems execute only authorized actions.

11–12 · Measure & Learn

11 Measure customer and business outcomes and guardrails → 12 store validated experiments, decisions, calibration and knowledge with provenance. Outcomes re-enter governed data.
